## 1. Project Overview

This project performs customer segmentation using transactional retail data.

The main objective is to divide customers into meaningful groups based on their purchasing behaviour. Recency, Frequency and Monetary (RFM) features are calculated from customer transaction data, and K-Means clustering is used to identify groups of customers with similar purchasing patterns.

The identified customer segments can help businesses understand customer behaviour and develop targeted marketing strategies.

---

## 2. Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour.
- Calculate Recency, Frequency and Monetary (RFM) features.
- Calculate average purchase value.
- Calculate purchase frequency.
- Analyse customer monetary value.
- Standardize RFM features.
- Determine the optimal number of clusters using the Elbow Method.
- Apply K-Means clustering.
- Evaluate clustering using Silhouette Score.
- Visualize customer clusters.
- Profile each customer segment.
- Identify the number of customers in each segment.
- Provide marketing recommendations for each segment.

6. Data Cleaning
The dataset was inspected for missing values, duplicate records and invalid transactions.
Customer records without a CustomerID were removed because CustomerID is required for customer-level segmentation.
Duplicate records were removed to avoid repeated transactions affecting the analysis.
The InvoiceDate column was converted into datetime format.
Cancelled transactions were removed by identifying invoice numbers beginning with the letter C.
Transactions with zero or negative quantities were removed.
Transactions with zero or negative unit prices were also removed.

7. Feature Engineering
A new column called TotalAmount was created to calculate the total value of each transaction.
The calculation used was:

TotalAmount = Quantity × UnitPrice

This value represents the amount spent in each transaction.

8. RFM Analysis
RFM analysis was used to understand customer purchasing behaviour.
Recency
Recency measures how recently a customer made a purchase.
A lower Recency value means that the customer purchased more recently.
Frequency
Frequency measures how often a customer made purchases.
A higher Frequency value indicates more frequent purchasing behaviour.
Monetary
Monetary measures the total amount spent by a customer.
A higher Monetary value indicates a higher-value customer.

12. Data Standardization
Before applying K-Means clustering, the RFM features were standardized using StandardScaler.
Standardization was performed because Recency, Frequency and Monetary values have different numerical scales.
The standardized features were then used as input for the K-Means clustering algorithm.

13. Elbow Method
The Elbow Method was used to determine a suitable number of customer clusters.
Different values of K were tested and the inertia value was calculated for each K.
Selected Number of Clusters
K = 4
The elbow point in the graph was used to select the appropriate number of clusters for the analysis.

14. K-Means Clustering
K-Means clustering was applied to the standardized RFM features.
The selected number of clusters was:

K = 4

Each customer was assigned to one of the identified clusters based on similarities in their purchasing behaviour.

18. Customer Segment Interpretation
The customer clusters were interpreted based on their Recency, Frequency and Monetary characteristics.
High Value Loyal Customers
Customers with relatively low Recency and high Frequency and Monetary values can be considered high-value loyal customers.
These customers purchase frequently and contribute significant revenue.
Recent Customers
Customers with low Recency values but relatively lower purchasing frequency can represent recent or newly active customers.
These customers have potential to become loyal customers.
Occasional Customers
Customers with moderate or lower purchasing frequency and monetary value can be considered occasional customers.
These customers may require targeted promotions to increase their purchase frequency.
At-Risk / Low-Value Customers
Customers with high Recency and low Frequency and Monetary values may represent customers who have not purchased recently and contribute lower revenue.
These customers can be targeted using re-engagement campaigns.
The final customer segment names should be based on the actual RFM values obtained from the clustering results.
